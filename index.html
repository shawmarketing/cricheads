My team & kitty

Squad size is the total players per team, retained players included. With squad size and the minimum bid filled in, the app shows the most each team can bid on the next player and still fill its squad.

My retained players

Pre-signed players with their price, which is taken off your kitty straight away.

Other teams

Starting kitty is what the team has at the start of the auction, after its pre-signed players have been paid for. Write the pre-signed names exactly as they appear in the player file, separated by commas.

TeamOwnerStarting kitty (₹)Pre-signed players

Player file

Excel or CSV with Name, Base Price and Target Price. A Role column is optional. If a player has no target price, they count as not on your list. Uploading a new version replaces the list, and every sale already recorded is kept.

Wish list file (optional)

Excel or CSV with Name and Target. These players appear first on the wish list, in the file's order, and their targets override the player file. Players with a target in the player file are added automatically.

Cloud sync (GitHub)

Keeps the same data on every device. This is already set up if the chip at the top says “Synced”.

Logins

Extra logins see only the Auction screen, with the same live data, and can record sales the same way. They cannot see or open Setup.

Data

Backup / restore everything as a file. Reset auction clears every recorded sale and unsold mark. Teams, kitties, retained players and files stay.
